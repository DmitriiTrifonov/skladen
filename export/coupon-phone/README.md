# Phone-fit coupons

Short pieces of the lid, printed to try the real phone (Pocophone F1) in before the
whole lid is printed. Generated by `cad/build.py`; the 3MF files are already laid in
the printing pose (rear wall on the bed, screen side up, no supports).

Each coupon is the bezel lid's left end, X from the lid's end to +24 mm. It has the
headphone-jack slot, the camera cutout, the button area with its tongues, the
pocket's corner and the crush ribs on the walls. The frame (bezel plate) is not part
of it. The phone is 155 mm long, so it sticks out of the coupon on the right: only
the left end holds it along its length, and the ribs on the long walls hold it
across.

## Rib height (ADR-0015)

The first print (`-ribs`, 0.40) left the phone loose. Print one of these instead.
Squeeze is the rib height less the 0.3 mm per-side pocket gap, with the phone centred.

| File | Rib height | Squeeze per side (calculated) |
|---|---|---|
| `skladen2-coupon-phone-lid-ribs.3mf` | 0.40 mm | 0.1 mm (printed, phone still loose) |
| `skladen2-coupon-phone-lid-ribs-h0.50.3mf` | 0.50 mm | 0.2 mm |
| `skladen2-coupon-phone-lid-ribs-h0.60.3mf` | 0.60 mm | 0.3 mm |
| `skladen2-coupon-phone-lid-ribs-h0.70.3mf` | 0.70 mm | 0.4 mm |

Start with 0.60. If the phone goes in only with force, or not at all, take 0.50; if it
still moves, 0.70. Then set `phone_rib_h` in `cad/params.py` to the winner and rebuild.
These coupons all carry the default button tongue, 1.0 mm. The old `-ribs` (0.40) file
is not on a plate.

## Button tongue thickness (ADR-0016)

Tongues of three thicknesses, to pick the push by feel. These are at rib height **0.60**
(the one to try first), so the 1.0 tongue is the `-ribs-h0.60` coupon. The stiffness
figures are a rough beam estimate (PETG, 10 mm tall tongue, at the tip), not a measurement.

| File | Tongue thickness | Stiffness (estimate) |
|---|---|---|
| `skladen2-coupon-phone-lid-ribs-tab0.8.3mf` | 0.8 mm | ~10 N/mm |
| `skladen2-coupon-phone-lid-ribs-h0.60.3mf` | 1.0 mm | ~19 N/mm |
| `skladen2-coupon-phone-lid-ribs-tab1.2.3mf` | 1.2 mm | ~33 N/mm |

Too soft: the lid's own weight presses a key. Too stiff: the key does not move.

## Plates

`skladen2-coupon-phone-plates.3mf` is a Bambu project of the coupons not printed yet, two to
a plate, each plate named: 1 = ribs 0.50 + ribs 0.70 (tongue 1.0), 2 = tongue 0.8 + tongue
1.2 (ribs 0.60). Left out: ribs 0.40 and `-h0.60`, which were printed first. The whole lids
in the same variants are plates 6 to 10 of `../skladen2-2.1-plates.3mf`, see
[`../lid-variants/`](../lid-variants/).
